## **FIFA — Where the World Becomes One**

From crowded streets to stadium lights,
A ball rolls on through dreams and nights.
Different flags, a thousand songs,
For ninety minutes, all belong.

One perfect pass, one fearless run,
A roaring crowd, a match begun.
A keeper flies, a striker dares,
And millions rise from homes and chairs.

Brazil brings rhythm, France brings flair,
Argentina’s passion fills the air.
From Asia’s hope to Africa’s pride,
The beautiful game has no divide.

A final whistle—joy and pain,
Some lift the Cup, some dream again.
For legends fade, but dreams survive,
And every four years, they come alive.

It’s more than goals, more than a game,
More than trophies, stars, and fame.
When nations meet beneath one sky,
The world speaks football—

**FIFA.**
